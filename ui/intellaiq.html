The problem

Your data is full of signals. Your teams are full of decisions.

Modern organisations have more information than ever. Every system produces another view. The problem is not a lack of information. It is knowing what matters, what changed, what is connected, what deserves attention and what to do about it.

The product

IntellaIQ turns information into judgement.

IntellaIQ continuously examines the signals around your business and connects the ones that belong together. It looks for meaningful change, emerging patterns, contradictions, relationships and shifts in demand. Then it applies judgement to determine which signals deserve attention.

It is not another place to look at your data. It is a system for deciding what your data means.

Learning

Decisions create evidence too.

IntellaIQ does not stop when a decision is made. It records what happened next. Before an intervention: what do we believe? After an intervention: what actually happened? The system compares the two. The result becomes new evidence for future decisions.

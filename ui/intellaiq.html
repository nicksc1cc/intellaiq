One brand. Multiple realities.

A brand exists simultaneously in its own content, in human experience, in the market and inside machine-generated understanding. These realities rarely align. The system brings them into a single model.

What the intelligence sees.

Every relationship is a computational fact, not a decorative connection. The graph stores what each entity means, how it relates to others and what evidence supports each relationship.

Not everything worth doing is equally worth doing.

Brands have more potential interventions than resources. The system evaluates each against expected impact, evidence, confidence, effort, risk and commercial relevance — and exposes the reasoning rather than collapsing it into a single number.

Impact is a hypothesis until the system observes what happened.

Every intervention produces an expected impact and an observed impact. The difference between them is where the system learns.

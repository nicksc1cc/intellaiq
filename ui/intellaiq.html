What is IntellaIQ?

An always-on research and decision system for your business.

IntellaIQ connects to the information your organisation already has. Give it a difficult business question and it doesn't simply return a summary. It investigates the problem.

It determines what information is relevant, generates the questions required to answer it, gathers evidence, tests competing explanations, identifies contradictions, evaluates uncertainty, and produces a defensible judgement.

IntellaIQ performs the investigation. Your people make the decision.

The process

From question to decision.

IntellaIQ works in five stages — from asking a question to producing a decision-ready investigation.

01

Ask

Give IntellaIQ a business question. It determines what information is relevant to the problem.

02

Investigate

It generates the specific questions required and gathers evidence from every relevant source.

03

Test

It constructs competing explanations, tests each against the evidence, and identifies contradictions.

04

Judge

It evaluates what the evidence supports, what contradicts it, and how uncertain the conclusion remains.

05

Decide

It identifies the decisions and actions worth considering — and what would change the conclusion.

JEV Framework

Every conclusion has to earn its confidence.

IntellaIQ uses the JEV framework — Judgement, Evidence, Verification — to ensure every conclusion is defensible.
